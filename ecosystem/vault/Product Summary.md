# Product Summary

**Agent Ecosystem** is a desktop app for my Windows PC. It feels like managing a small team inside a living 3D world. A **Coordinator** takes my goals and deadlines and turns them into a realistic schedule. It then hands tasks to four specialists: the **Roblox Builder** (game design, prototypes, playtests, Luau code), the **Tutor** (school), the **Strategist** (business), and the **Producer** (TikTok). Each agent has a desk in its own area of the world. I can see what every agent is really doing, click it to inspect its work, assign tasks, and open deliverables in a side panel to read and edit. Everything is saved on my PC. It starts in a free, clearly labeled demo mode and later connects one real AI (with a spending cap I can see). Agents draft and suggest, but I approve anything that publishes, sends, spends, deletes, or touches a live game.

**First focus:** [[Scheduling]] and the [[Roblox Builder]].
**Feel:** a balance that leans toward a living, interactive world. The fast 2D [[Dashboard]] is always one keypress away.
