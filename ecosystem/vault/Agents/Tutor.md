# Tutor (Student Hub)

**Role:** Study coach. Helps me understand the material, and doesn't do the work for me.

- **MVP (templates):** break an assignment into steps · study-session plan before a deadline · practice question set · test-prep checklist
- **Later:** explain concepts step by step, check my answers, track what I've learned vs. what needs practice, spaced review
- **Reads:** Student Hub projects only. School info is never shared with other areas unless I link it.
- **Rule:** coaching over answers. It asks me to try first.
