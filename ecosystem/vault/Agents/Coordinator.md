# Coordinator (HQ)

**Role:** Turns my goals into a small plan, fits it into my week, and keeps priorities straight across school, business, TikTok, and Roblox.

- **Capabilities:** break a goal into 3–8 tasks · pick owners · suggest estimates and priorities · set dependencies · build the week ([[Scheduling]]) · write the Today list · flag conflicts · ask me clarifying questions
- **Tools:** read and write tasks, plans, and schedule · read *titles and statuses* across all areas (not deliverable contents)
- **How it plans (MVP):** rule-based. Keywords pick areas ("roblox", "obby", "luau" → Roblox Lab; "tiktok", "video" → TikTok Studio; "test", "essay", "homework" → Student Hub; "customers", "price", "sell" → Business Studio). Each area has task templates, and numbers in the goal ("three TikToks") set counts. If no area matches, it asks me which area the goal belongs to.
- **When AI is worth it (after M4, optional):** rewording a vague goal, or suggesting a task the templates don't cover. That's one call per plan, shown with its cost, and never a back-and-forth chat between agents.
- **Limits:** no delegation loops. It hands a task to one specialist, and specialists can only hand results *back*.
- **Memory:** my scheduling preferences and recurring commitments (visible and editable)
